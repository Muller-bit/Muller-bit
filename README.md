### 👋 About me

I'm a Senior Operations Manager with a B.Sc. in Computer Science and an MSc in Economics & Finance — currently building deep expertise in **cybersecurity, cloud security, and GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance)**.

My background spans running business operations, automating workflows with Python and SQL, and managing organizational risk — and I'm now applying that same rigor to information security, identity & access management, and regulatory frameworks like **GDPR, ISO 27001, and DORA**.

I write and build in public because I believe the transition into cybersecurity is more useful to document honestly than to perform.

<br/>

### 🎯 Currently

- 🔐 Studying toward **CompTIA Security+**, **Microsoft SC-900**, and **AZ-500**
- 📚 Building hands-on GRC and IAM/PAM knowledge — access control, identity governance, technology risk
- ✍️ Writing weekly on GRC, cybersecurity, and blockchain risk on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mulualem-darimo-2025621ba/)
